# TampaBayBot risk enumeration

| # | Risk | Suggested warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Incomplete resource discovery | **Results may not be complete.** TampaBayBot searches the sources currently in its coverage, but other programs or resources may exist. |
| **2** | Stale information | **This information may have changed.** Check the source date below and confirm current requirements with the responsible agency before acting. |
| **3** | False eligibility implication | **A match does not mean you qualify.** TampaBayBot can identify programs that may be relevant, but the responsible agency determines eligibility. |
| **4** | False ineligibility implication | **No match does not mean no help is available.** TampaBayBot may not cover every program or circumstance. Consider checking with the responsible agency or a housing-resource specialist. |
| **5** | Wrong jurisdiction | **Location matters.** Programs and rules can change across city and county boundaries. Confirm the location shown below before relying on this information. |
| **6** | Source applicability | **This source may not apply everywhere in Tampa Bay.** City, county and regional agencies have different service areas and authority. Check the jurisdiction shown with each result. |
| **7** | Citation lacks important context | **Read the source for full details.** The evidence shown here may be only part of a longer rule, program description or set of exceptions. |
| **8** | Conflicting sources | **The available sources disagree.** TampaBayBot found conflicting information and cannot reliably resolve it. Confirm the current rule or requirement with the responsible agency. |
| **9** | Apparent comprehensiveness | **TampaBayBot does not search every possible source.** Results reflect the sources and geographic areas currently covered by the project. |
| **10** | Apparent government authority | **TampaBayBot is an independent project.** It is not operated by or affiliated with the City of Tampa or other Tampa Bay governments. |
| **11** | Excessive confidence | **This answer is informational, not an official determination.** Where the available evidence is uncertain or incomplete, confirm the answer with the responsible agency. |
| **12** | Legal interpretation | **This is not a legal or permitting determination.** Zoning, land-use and permitting information can help you understand public records, but the responsible authority determines what is legally permitted. |
