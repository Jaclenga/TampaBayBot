# Static housing resource directory

`housing-resources.json` is bundled into Cloudflare Pages assets. Reading it needs no API, account, Workers AI, embedding, or external search. The directory is a selected navigation list for Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco; it is not a complete inventory of assistance.

## What verification means

- `verifiedAt` records when a contributor checked the named public source page and its contact or service details. `verificationStatus: source_checked` means only that the page was checked. It does **not** verify live staff response, shelter space, an open waitlist, an appointment, available funds, an application decision, or personal eligibility.
- `availabilityStatus` is separate from source verification. Every current record is `unknown` because live capacity and funding were not confirmed with a provider. The schema permits `confirmed_open`, `temporarily_closed`, and `discontinued`; contributors must cite and date direct evidence before changing a status.
- `refreshDays` is 30 for changeable intake, contact, and service pages, and 90 for stable court/legal reference pages. A record older than that is stale even if it was `source_checked` before. The interface must flag it and direct the visitor to the live official page. The plan builder prefers recently checked records. If none match, an older contact checked within 365 days may appear with `needs_recheck` status and an explicit instruction to confirm its official source; closed and unavailable-source records remain excluded. The static directory remains browsable.
- `crisisCategories` are reviewed routing tags, not an eligibility finding. `municipalities` narrows city programs. An unincorporated county code office should never be described as a city code office.
- `intakeUrl` appears only when the official source links to a direct intake path and no unresolved conflict affects it. `serviceHours` appear only where the provider publishes them. Both can change; visitors should check the live source.
- `restrictions` carries important geography, referral, privacy, and source-conflict cautions. Do not strip them from cards or copied plans.

Before publishing or after `refreshDays` expires, open the `url` and `sourceUrl`, check the precise contact, coverage, eligibility, hours, and intake route, then update `verifiedAt`. If the source cannot be checked, set `verificationStatus: needs_recheck` or `source_unavailable`; do not advance the date. Do not convert a provider's general program description into a claim that funds or beds are open. A phone call that directly verifies current availability may justify a time-limited `confirmed_open` status only with an internal audit record and an expiry; this release has no such record.

## Significant source checks on 2026-10-09

- [Bay Area Legal Services' help page](https://bals.org/help) lists eligible civil legal help, the Legal Aid Line, service hours, and a linked online intake. Its [locations page](https://bals.org/locations) establishes the three local counties in its wider service area. Case acceptance remains an agency decision.
- [Florida Courts' self-help page](https://www.flcourts.gov/Services/Family-Courts/self-help-information) includes landlord-tenant links; its [getting started guidance](https://www.flcourts.gov/Services/family-courts/self-help-information/getting-started) limits the published phone to form/access questions and says staff cannot give legal advice. The [Hillsborough](https://www.hillsclerk.com/eviction), [Pinellas](https://www.mypinellasclerk.gov/Portals/0/Forms/Tenant_Info_1.pdf), and [Pasco](https://pascoclerk.com/700/LandlordTenant-Eviction) clerk entries provide county-specific court paths. Do not calculate a case deadline from a general web page.
- [Pinellas AEFAP](https://pinellas.gov/adult-emergency-financial-assistance-program) describes past-due rent and utility needs for adults without minor children, with 211 screening. [Pinellas FHAP](https://pinellas.gov/programs/family-housing-assistance-program) describes help for families experiencing homelessness **after Coordinated Entry referral**. Neither source proves a funded slot for a visitor.
- [Hillsborough Social Services](https://hcfl.gov/departments/social-services) and [Help Me, Hillsborough](https://hcfl.gov/residents/human-services/help-me-hillsborough) provide housing navigation and a public call center. No current official page checked for this release established an open **county** grant specifically paying rent arrears on an existing lease. The [Hillsborough 211 provider](https://www.crisiscenter.com/about-us/services-programs/gateway-contact-center/) offers current referrals, not a payment promise.
- [Metropolitan Ministries' Get Help page](https://www.metromin.org/get-help/) describes rent, mortgage, and utility assistance on a first-come basis while funds last. Its [Hillsborough](https://www.metromin.org/financial-assistance-for-hillsborough-county/) and [Pasco](https://www.metromin.org/financial-assistance-for-pasco-county/) pages require in-person orientation; its [Pinellas](https://www.metromin.org/financial-assistance-in-pinellas-county/) page lists a Friday virtual orientation in English. Orientations, appointment slots, funding, and approvals are not verified here. The provider's [shelter intake portal](https://metrominintake.powerappsportals.com/) requires registration and collects application information; calling county intake is an alternative. The directory does not collect an application.
- The [City of Tampa RMAP program page](https://www.tampa.gov/housing-and-community-development/rental-move-in-assistance), updated 2026-09-17, says Phase X is **new move-in costs only** and excludes existing leases. Its [official linked Phase X portal](https://apps.tampagov.net/My_TampaGov_WebApp/Housing/PhaseX.aspx) still contains current-lease and eviction-prevention language. Until city staff resolves that conflict, RMAP is excluded from eviction and rent-arrears routing and its direct intake URL is withheld from the directory. Ask the city Housing Information Line before relying on either description.
- [Tampa Housing Authority](https://www.tampaha.org/) publishes its help-center number and links to [wait-list information](https://www.tampaha.org/wait-list-information). [Florida Housing](https://extranet.floridahousing.org/programs/renters) links the [statewide rental locator](https://www.floridahousingsearch.org/). An individual vacancy or open waitlist is not checked here.
- Unsafe housing contacts use [City of Tampa Neighborhood Enhancement](https://www.tampa.gov/neighborhood-enhancement/contact-us), [unincorporated Hillsborough Code Enforcement](https://hcfl.gov/residents/property-owners-and-renters/code-violations/report-a-code-violation), [Pinellas renter complaint routing](https://rent.pinellas.gov/help-with-housing/), and [Pasco County customer-service routing](https://pascocountyfl.qscend.com/311/contact). Hillsborough says code complaints cannot be anonymous and the complainant's name/address may be a public record. Ask the office about confidentiality before disclosing sensitive details. Code enforcement is not emergency dispatch.
- [THHI's homeless resource page](https://www.thhi.org/homelessresources) links a meal-site map and resource guide. [Metropolitan Ministries](https://www.metromin.org/get-help/) lists county-specific shelter-intake and Metro BrigAIDe mobile-outreach numbers; the outreach team describes housing assessments, referrals, and identification help. [Pasco's coalition](https://www.pascohomelesscoalition.org/services/) describes coordinated entry. [Pinellas First Contact](https://www.firstcontact.org/homeless-helpline) and [Hillsborough 211](https://www.crisiscenter.com/about-us/services-programs/gateway-contact-center/) provide local referral paths. These pages do not prove a particular opening, meeting point, or meal service at a particular time.
- [911.gov](https://www.911.gov/), [SAMHSA 988](https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/988), [The Spring](https://thespring.org/get-help/crisis-hotline/), [CASA Pinellas](https://casapinellas.org/get-help/), and [Sunrise of Pasco](https://www.sunrisepasco.org/) provide emergency and domestic-violence contacts. [FEMA's application guidance](https://www.fema.gov/node/4-ways-apply-disaster-assistance) names the disaster-assistance channel. No source here confirms a specific shelter bed or disaster application window.
- Cloudflare's [official model catalog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/) and [model page](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/llama-3.1-8b-instruct-fp8/) still list the preferred `@cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct-fp8` model as Cloudflare-hosted. The static directory does not call it.

The summaries are short factual descriptions written for this project. They are not copies of provider pages. Recheck every record against its official source periodically and ask providers directly about current capacity, funds, and intake before presenting any of those details as available.
